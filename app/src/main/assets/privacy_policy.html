Privacy Policy for AstroVeda

Effective Date: August 29, 2026

AstroVeda ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of our users. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use the AstroVeda Android application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Sign-In Data: When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your basic profile information (Email address, Display Name, and Profile Picture URL).
  • Astrological & Birth Details: Name, Date of Birth, Time of Birth, and Place of Birth entered for generating Kundali, Horoscope, and Matching reports.
  • Device Location: GPS location data is accessed locally on your device solely to calculate accurate, location-specific Panchang, Rahu Kaal, and planetary rise/set times. Location data is processed on-device and is not sold or tracked.
  • Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to deliver requested daily Panchang alerts and planetary transit notifications.
  • Crash Logs & Diagnostics: Anonymous system diagnostics and crash reports to maintain application stability and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To compute accurate Vedic astrology reports (Kundali, Panchang, Dasha, Match Making).
  • To synchronize your saved birth profiles across devices via cloud backup (Firestore) when signed in.
  • To send opted-in notifications regarding daily astrological timings and transit alerts.
  • To improve application features, fix bugs, and ensure security.

2a. AI Astrologer

The "Ask AI Astrologer" feature is optional and only runs when you type a question and send it. When you do, your question is sent to Google's Generative Language API (Gemini) so an answer can be generated. If you have a birth chart open at the time, the birth details shown on that chart (name, date of birth, time of birth and place of birth) are sent with the question so the answer can refer to them.

If you never use this feature, none of your data is ever sent to Gemini. The astrological calculations — Panchang, Kundali, Dasha, Matching and Numerology — all run on your device and need no internet connection at all.

AI answers are guidance, not professional advice, and can be wrong. AstroVeda does not give medical, legal or financial advice. If you are in distress, Tele-MANAS (14416) offers free confidential support in Indian languages, 24x7.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your saved profiles are stored locally in a Room database on your device, inside the app's private storage which other apps cannot read. They are excluded from Android system backups, so your birth details are not copied to Google Drive or transferred to a new device automatically. For signed-in users, profiles are backed up to secure Google Firebase / Firestore cloud infrastructure using SSL/TLS encryption in transit and AES encryption at rest. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account remains active. You can delete your saved profiles at any time. Signed-in users can request full account and cloud data deletion by selecting "Delete My Account & Data" under Settings in the app, or by contacting our support team.

5. Children's Privacy

AstroVeda is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

  • Firebase Authentication, Firestore & Cloud Messaging
  • Google AdMob (for non-Pro users)
