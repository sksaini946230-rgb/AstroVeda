# Privacy Policy for AstroVeda

**Effective Date:** July 24, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 25, 2026  

AstroVeda ("we", "our", or "us", operated by Msunjay Enterprises) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards data when you use the AstroVeda Android mobile application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

* **Account Information:** When you use Google Sign-In, we receive your basic public profile information (Name and Email address) solely to authenticate and synchronize your birth profiles across devices.
* **Astrological Data:** Name, Date of Birth, Time of Birth, and Place of Birth entered by you to generate Janam Kundali, Dasha, Horoscope, and Ashtakoot Match-Making charts.
* **Location Data:** Coarse/Approximate device GPS location is used locally on your device solely to calculate accurate, location-specific astronomical Sunrise, Sunset, Rahu Kaal, and Choghadiya timings. Location data is never tracked, sold, or shared with third parties.
* **Diagnostics & Performance:** Anonymous crash logs and performance telemetry via Firebase Crashlytics to detect bugs and optimize app stability.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Data

* To compute accurate Vedic astrology calculations locally on your device.
* To provide cloud backup for your saved birth profiles.
* To deliver opted-in daily Panchang and auspicious Muhurat notifications.
* To continuously improve app security, performance, and user experience.

---

## 3. Data Storage & Security

* All sensitive calculations are computed on-device using local astronomical algorithms.
* Data transmitted to Firebase/Cloud is encrypted in transit (HTTPS / TLS 1.3) and encrypted at rest (AES-256).
* We **never** sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party advertisers.

---

## 4. User Rights & Data Deletion

You retain full control over your data:
* You can delete any saved Kundali profile at any time directly in the app.
* You can request complete account and data deletion by clicking **"Delete Account & Data"** in the app settings, or by emailing us at `support@astroveda.app`.
